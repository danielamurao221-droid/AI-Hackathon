Priorities

Active tasks ranked by how soon they're due and how much they matter.

How the ranking works. Each task gets urgency points from its due date (overdue scores highest, then today, tomorrow, this week, and later) plus importance points (high, medium, low). Higher totals rank first. When two tasks tie, the one due sooner wins. Drag a task by its handle to set your own order; new tasks are slotted in by their score.
